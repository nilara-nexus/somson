FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I service my AC?
For regular home use, professional maintenance every 6 to 12 months is commonly recommended. Usage, environment and AC condition can change the ideal interval.
How do I know which AC capacity I need?
Room size, sunlight, ceiling height, insulation, number of people and heat-generating equipment can affect the required capacity.
Why is my AC not cooling properly?
Common causes include dirty filters, blocked airflow, coil contamination, refrigerant issues or electrical problems. A technician can inspect the system.
Can I book an AC service online?
Yes. Use the Book Service button and submit your details through the booking form.
